A suggested process

Clear expectations help everyone.

A small, specific starting scope can make it easier to decide whether the arrangement works for both sides.

Share the tasks

Describe what needs doing, how often it happens, and what a good result looks like.

Confirm fit and scope

Discuss which tasks are available, what is included, timing, rates, tools, and any exclusions.

Agree on communication

Set the channel, update cadence, approval points, and who to contact for urgent questions.

Review and adjust

Check in after the agreed period and make changes together if the priorities shift.

Before sharing access

Keep your information and accounts protected.

Agree on secure access methods and only share the information needed for the approved tasks. Confirm confidentiality and data-handling expectations before work begins.
