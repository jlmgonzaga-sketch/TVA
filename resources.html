A practical guide

How to prepare a task for delegation.

A clear brief helps a virtual assistant understand the outcome, make fewer assumptions, and know when to ask for help.

The Virtual Answer

A smoother handoff begins with a clear brief

Use these prompts to document the task, expected result, and escalation steps.

The delegation checklist

Before handing over a task, write down these six things.

The result

What should be finished, and how will you know it is done correctly?

The deadline and frequency

When is it due? Is it one-time, daily, weekly, or triggered by an event?

The steps and examples

Share the process, a good example, and common exceptions to watch for.

The tools and access

List the systems needed and provide access only through an agreed secure method.

The decision boundaries

Explain what can be handled independently and what needs your approval.

The update plan

Choose how progress, questions, and completed work should be reported.

Try it with one recurring task

Start small, then improve the handoff.

After the first run, note what was unclear and update your instructions. A short review can make the next handoff smoother.
